# Study 14 — broken earth and painted colonies

The original concept illustration remains the visual target. Ground cover now uses original generated brush-painted artwork: low sage tufts, dry ochre tussocks and rusty clay fragments. These grow in uneven colonies with bare spaces, replacing the previous thin blades and scattered triangular scrub. Terrain has broken dusty patches and darker clay marks. Existing roads, car contact, rider animation, controls, sky and sound are unchanged.

Validation: standalone build 22,104,513 bytes; entrance and walking-path rendering checked; 2,646 clumps batched across four species; minimum plant-center road clearance 4.02 m and walking-path clearance 1.85 m. Finite shadow matrix, no browser rendering errors. Actual Android performance remains for device review.

Art: original generated ground-cover atlas, directed against the project's concept illustration. Existing asset credits and licenses embedded in the build remain applicable.
