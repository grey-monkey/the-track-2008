# Board asset review — Study 42

Four separate downloaded models, adapted for the existing game. Ratings below are editorial judgments out of five, based on inspecting the source meshes and the adapted in-game result; they are not marketplace ratings. Weighting: silhouette/era fit 30%, visible mechanical construction 30%, material potential 20%, phone efficiency 20%.

| Role / source | Shape & fit | Construction | Materials | Phone efficiency | Weighted score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Nice: elmaterdor Drop-Through Longboard | 4.5 | 4.5 | 4 | 3.5 | 4.2 |
| Hand-me-down: elmaterdor Longboard | 4 | 4 | 3.5 | 4.5 | 4.0 |
| Bowl cruiser: elmaterdor Cruiser Board | 4.5 | 4.5 | 4 | 4.5 | 4.4 |
| Street: urnanscreditcard Element skateboard | 4.5 | 4 | 4 | 4.5 | 4.25 |

The nice board is a generic 105 cm drop-through with 66 mm wheels and visible reverse-kingpin truck construction. It suggests a sensible enthusiast board, not an exotic race setup. The 98 cm hand-me-down has a conventional rounded longboard outline, muted wood and worn grip, and 65 mm wheels; it is Sector 9-like in role, not a claimed Sector 9 replica. The 80 cm cruiser has a broad nose, shaped kicktail and 62 mm amber wheels. The 79 cm Element board uses the downloaded deck artwork and geometry, with 52 mm wheels. Dimensions are adaptation targets rather than manufacturer-certified specifications.

Adaptations: scale, wheel diameter, material treatment, texture resizing and game integration. Trademark/graphic is part of the downloaded reference model, not an endorsement.

The downloaded Blender models contain roughly 160k–245k evaluated source triangles including tiny screw and bearing details. Optimized versions retain 20,058 / 7,830 / 9,184 triangles respectively; the Element has 8,594. Retained: deck concavity and kicks, hangers, baseplates, bushings, nuts, four wheels. Removed/reduced: invisible bearing balls and subpixel screw threads. Replacement charcoal grip and muted finish replace neon source colors. Element underside artwork retained at 512 px.

Also considered: BlendSwap Stellarator skateboard (24364), archived fully rigged skateboard (15077), Koowheel-derived electric longboard (21193), Sketchfab Nikita Kravchenko and Kaden Cooley longboards, low-poly retro models, and marketplace paid options. Electric and plastic Penny shapes did not fit the requested memory. The Element model gives a more direct match for the final street board than another generic deck. No paid assets or subscriptions purchased.

Validation: all four boards inspected from above and the Element from below; foot-height comparison with native skater; contact verification using the actual adapted meshes across eight tumbling orientations on a sloped, uneven surface, minimum clearance 2 mm. Desktop contact-only timings are about 0.3–1.4 ms median, not phone frame-rate claims. Physical-phone ride-through remains the final performance check.
