# Study 17 — Quieter crew, purple beanie

Sprad now wears a close-fitting muted purple beanie, bound to his head for the seated look-back pose. Removed the opening rope handoff and the friend walking over after a fall. Rides start immediately; passengers remain seated and look back. Removed the special post-fall camera framing. Woods, terrain, car, riding physics and the existing rider bodies are unchanged.

Validation: standalone game loads and starts without browser errors; inspected Sprad close-up and in the passenger seat; exercised the stopped-car review without the removed helper. The replacement character research below is not integrated into this build.

## Character research — September 11, 2026

Compared the published previews against the original illustration: natural teenage proportions, distinct hair silhouette, loose clothing, broad painted folds, understated faces, and believable skating poses.

- **Otaku — Richie Kirui, BlendKit:** strongest starting point among the inspected complete human models. Free, uploader lists Royalty Free; 41,012 polygons, rigged with facial blendshapes. Its silhouette is less toy-like, but clothing, shoes, hair, textures and proportions need adaptation. Rig deformation and mobile performance have not been tested locally. https://www.blendkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/c5864069-4183-41dc-b80f-5cedb9cef7c8/
- **Koi Boy — nottodayrender, Sketchfab:** useful painted clothing reference, not a human replacement. Broad fabric folds and painted shadow shapes demonstrate the desired surface treatment. Free CC BY 4.0 as listed by uploader; 15.4k triangles. https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/koi-boy-3d-character-in-handpainted-style-8811c961c2e7492db591aa2a676eb224
- **Lowpoly Stylized Skater Boy — Afonso Rodenbusch:** free CC BY, 11.2k triangles; inspected and rejected for this project because the oversized head, hands and shoes make it too toy-like. https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lowpoly-stylized-skater-boy-07e815c49f514a8093237cc17bf23b91
- **Symon — Alexey Brodsky, BlendKit:** free and rigged, but cartoon proportions are too exaggerated. https://www.blendkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/600db9c8-a249-403f-b5d7-f47089011796/
- **CharCrafter free sample — Ayuo Dev:** five rigged characters, mobile-oriented, free personal/commercial project use; no redistribution of source assets. The faceless angular style repeats the mannequin problem, so not recommended. https://ayuo-dev.itch.io/charcrafter-free-stylized-character-sample-pack
- **Boy Finnigon — Fion Noir:** CC BY, painted model with facial/body rig referenced separately inside the blend file. Expressive face, but anime styling and separate old rig dependency make it a weaker fit. https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/boy-finnigon-lowpoly-gamecharacter-990be0ecf1634d3b94c6e3b529cef211

Recommendation: trial one adapted Otaku-based rider in the actual rear gameplay camera before replacing the entire crew. Match the illustration's loose tee, shoulder slope, uneven hair outline, shorts folds, warm edge light and restrained painted shadows. Preserve recognizable differences from the original crew brief. No new purchased assets or subscriptions are needed for this trial. Do not treat an asset's preview as proof that its rig or style works in this game.
