ONE THUMB. THAT’S IT.

Feel the pull.

Drag left or right to lean into a carve.

Hold your thumb still to crouch and settle the board.

Lift after a hold for a trick: Sally can ollie on the Dogtown or Element. Black can ollie on the Dogtown and kickflip on the Element. Boss, Dog, and both longboards stay grounded.

Flick up to throw the rope. You’ll coast until the crew can regroup.

Momentum carries you wide. Carve through turns; crouch to settle wobble. On the pintail, load a hard carve at speed to slide; ease the steering to regain grip. Let go early if you’re losing it.

Keyboard: ← → carve · Space crouch / ollie · ↑ let go.
