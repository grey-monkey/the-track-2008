Date: 2026-10-16. Read-only research; no game-source modification.

OBSERVED
- 5,729 textures over29 level/environment archives. Top-level dimensions predominantly64x64,128x128 and rectangular128x64/64x128;256px is reserved for many recognizable ground/wood panels. The texture archive is not a universal64px pixel-art palette.
- Stored DXT-version1 and2 carry8 bytes per4x4 block (BC1/DXT1-compatible);5 carries16 (BC3-compatible); some sky textures have8-bit indices +BGRA palettes. BC1/BC3 previews decode coherent material detail, labels and architecture.
- Every sampled scene sector carries vertex colors: Sch952/952, Jnk1248/1248, Sk4Ed215/215. Normals occur in only15,4,0 sectors respectively. Their total248,845 vertices include extensive nonwhite RGB modulation. This is direct evidence for authored vertex shading, not an all-white diffuse scene.
- Sch629materials:497one-pass,103two-pass,19three-pass,10four-pass. Jnk809:607/132/58/12. Sk4Ed386:325/52/8/1. Selected ordinary concrete/plywood/asphalt/soil use flags0x24; source flag mapping names Textured +Smooth. Transparent overlays0x64 are also common.
- Selected nonlogo materials use UV addressing[0,0], filteringMode65540(0x10004), mip MMAG1/MMIN4/K-8/L-8, blend0, hasColor=false. Raw color triple(0.5,0.5,0.5) is retained but not asserted as a literal0.5 multiply when hasColor=false. Do not blindly darken the Track to half brightness.
- Nearly every material ambient/diffuse/specular serialized triple is allzero (2,347of2,350passes); three are uniformly0.5. Do not interpret those zero triples as proof the world has no lighting: vertex colors supply the strongest observed shading channel.

VISUAL OBSERVATIONS FROM DECODED CONTACT SHEETS
- Concrete and asphalt contain fine photographic aggregate at low source resolution, with broad value variation and stains. Wood has visible grain, knots, dark nail marks and panel-edge weathering.
- Narrow trim textures separately carry dirty base edges, rust rims, seams and curb transitions; separate alpha masks carry cracks, drips, scuffs and soft darkness. Whole-surface noisy color alone misses this authored hierarchy.
- Palette is material-specific: neutral cool concrete/asphalt, tan/orange wood, earthy gray-brown dirt, restrained colored painted metal. These are diffuse samples before scene vertex tint and runtime fog; not a final screen palette.
- Texture resolution should remain limited and filtered. Aggressive nearest-neighbor pixelation is not established by these files.

BUILD142 GUIDANCE — inference/design adaptation
1. Establish a consistent base texture scale across road/concrete/wood, then add panel seams, dark underedges and contact shading. Keep the Track peach/indigo sunset tint as an adaptation; avoid flattening every material to the same peach/navy solid.
2. Let large face/vertex shading express the ramp slope and underside; use diffuse+vertex colors rather than shiny PBR as the main look. Keep specularity low for dirt, asphalt and dry plywood.
3. Author panel sizes deliberately: plywood grain runs up the ramp; screws/seams follow supports; the underside is substantially darker. Ground marks should read as use, not evenly distributed random scratches.
4. Broad clay uses fine opaque soil/gravel plus slower color breakup. Avoid repeating alpha-border dirt patches over the entire ground.
5. The contact sheet establishes materials, not gameplay camera, native dynamic light positions, tonemapping, or final frame color. Those remain separate research questions.

UNKNOWNS / LIMITATIONS
- Texture semantic names in this report are visual classification, not recovered original names.
- Raw sampler enums above are exact observations; precise runtime interpretation of65540 andmipK/L is unconfirmed. Repeat is a practical interpretation of UV[0,0], not a verified render capture.
- Native scene lighting direction, fog curve, shader-stage math, monitor gamma, platform-specific compression differences and runtime texture LOD are not recovered here.
- No native THPS4 gameplay was launched or filmed; no claim of pixel-perfect screenshot match.
- This research does not establish redistribution rights. Local source provenance is preserved for any selected textures used by parent.
