TRACK ADAPTATION RECOMMENDATION [INFERENCE, NOT SOURCE BEHAVIOR]
Existing Track traction/heading is the authority. Do not blindly apply full native board yaw on top of the physically sideways board, or play the 180-degree clip to completion during a sustained slide. Use a limited pre-switch portion (BS approximately30-43% as slide loading grows), hold or smoothly vary with actual slide amount, and reverse that preparation on recovery. Neutralize the source board yaw in the whole pose around board contact before placing the body on Track's actual board orientation. Preserve source knee/hip/arm offsets relative to the board. The body and board must use the SAME corrected frame. For opposite slide direction, select the genuine paired source with constrained head look, or explicitly describe mirrored BS as an adaptation. Do not imply it is original FS behavior. Any mirror must preserve joint handedness and foot stance; do not merely negate Euler yaw on the head. Exact mirror method belongs to retarget implementation and visual QA.
Concrete yaw neutralization: deltaQ=sampledBoardQ*inverse(nativeBindBoardQ), yaw=atan2((deltaQ*Z).x,(deltaQ*Z).z), Q=Ry(-yaw); M=T(boardPosition)*Q*T(-boardPosition). Premultiply all world and skin matrices by M, transform joint positions with M, premultiply all joint rotations and board rotation by Q; board position stays fixed; update pelvis. Then standard anchoring and the physical board heading apply once. Head look should reference actual velocity after this correction. This is a Track adaptation, not recovered native runtime math.
Towing requires separate rope reach. A free-skating full-body powerslide must not break the holding hand; retain the rope hand solution if applied while towing.

These are full50-bone alternatives, NOT additive arm masks. They already existed in additions142, but Build143 always selected PushCycle. Activate original alternatives with a stable choice per push, not a new random choice every render frame. Sequence order/random distribution is Track adaptation unless native Random is reimplemented.
FK of21 evenly spaced times: Arms1..5 share ankle trajectories with one another, but maximum ankle difference from base PushCycle is6.2513 native units. Left pushing ankle minimumY occurs at15% (3.8301), base PushCycle minimum at10% (2.6985). The normal source plant foot is right; pushing foot is left before stance reflection.
Current Track physics applies push force at7-18% of.9s cycle; this broadly overlaps source contact, but original variations contact later. Recommend coordinating force/contact window with selected pose rather than introducing a disconnected constant force.
Push(.3s), StartSkatingPush(1.2667), PushCycleOut(.7333) decoded as candidates. No use of these three was found in the inspected decoded script set, so do not label them mandatory normal push transitions.
