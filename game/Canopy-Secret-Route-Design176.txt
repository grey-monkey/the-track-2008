THE CANOPY - SECRET ROUTE DESIGN
Status: design proposal informed by the supplied THPS4 artifacts; not implemented
in Build176. Boss's cosmetic low-speed wobble is the separate Build176 change.

USER-CONFIRMED WORLD
Entrance in the cul-de-sac, behind the existing large orange road barrier.
Barrier moves aside to expose a descending tunnel. The destination is The Canopy,
a remembered hangout: a round bistro table, four metal bistro chairs and a
retractable patio umbrella through its center. Concrete pavement, like sidewalk,
not asphalt. Enough clear space to skate circles around the furniture.
Almost complete darkness. Moonlight casts softly onto the bistro set and nearby
concrete; no lamps, neon or decorative glow. Secret Mixtape CD waits here.

PROPOSED PLAY SEQUENCE
1. Discover a weathered construction control box away from the entrance, near
   the residential works. Exact switch placement remains to be chosen in scene.
   A physical lever and worn cable conduit hint that it controls something.
2. Approach in free ride. A gentle, direction-aware brush past the lever flips
   it automatically; no new screen button, trick requirement or damaging impact.
   The interaction must have a generous safe approach and work on keyboard/touch.
3. Play a mechanical click and barrier scrape. A roughly 1.5-second camera reveal
   shows the orange barrier moving sideways and exposing a low concrete mouth.
   Freeze the simulation safely during this first reveal, including the car and
   evening clock; music continues. Return the camera to the rider smoothly.
4. Keep the passage open for this page's evening, through retries and rider
   changes. No timer, repeated cutscene or repeat unlock score farming.
5. Roll down the shallow concrete entry. Darkness hides a short scene transition
   to The Canopy. Preserve rider, board, stance, score, album playback and motion
   direction. Adapt speed only as needed for a safe entrance; do not drop the
   rider onto the furniture. The car stays on its original circuit.
6. A modest rotating CD at the tabletop is collected by skating past within
   reach, not by jumping onto the table. Existing white score-area text says
   SECRET MIXTAPE. Call the existing album unlock hook; preserve current music
   until the player deliberately selects the newly available album.
7. The same passage leads back. Another run after a Canopy crash restarts safely
   at its entrance; Back to the car returns to the existing selection flow.

ART DIRECTION
Use the existing game's material scale, grain and geometry language. The orange
barrier slides on a credible temporary track: reveal a pre-existing construction
opening rather than have a giant tunnel rise magically through intact pavement.
The mouth needs a concrete retaining surround, terrain cutout, correct shadows
and fitted collision. Its descent should be gentle and readable, not a launch ramp.
For The Canopy, start with a roughly 10-12 m concrete pad, a 0.9-1.0 m round table,
four slender metal chairs and a simple open 2 m umbrella. Dimensions are design
starting points, not a claim about the real location. Leave at least 2.5 m of
clear skating space outside chair backs. Subtle slab joints; no bumpy physics seams.
Cool, low moonlight grazes umbrella folds, chair rims and tabletop. Low-contrast
contact shadows ground the furniture. A very dim blue-gray floor wash keeps
steering readable on phones without making the scene brightly lit. Keep the
far surroundings black. No added score boxes, quest panels or neon arrows.

STATE AND ENGINE WORK
Use page-lifetime route state: locked -> opening -> open, plus active area
track/canopy. Store switch activation outside Ride so retries do not re-lock it.
The existing Secret Mixtape discovery is persistent album-library state; reuse
that behavior. Reload closes the route for a new evening, but an already found
album remains available. No points awarded repeatedly for the switch or CD.
The barrier collider must move with its visible mesh, and passage access starts
only when it has cleared. Use swept triggers so high frame times cannot skip entry.
Preload the destination before fading out. If loading fails, leave the player
on safe track pavement and restore control. Preserve audio and Settings access.
Use a dedicated flat concrete support/collision area in The Canopy. It is rideable
for all characters, including Boss; his usual track curb/dirt/obstacle rules are
unchanged outside this area. Table/chair colliders remain obstacles. No invented
ramps, tricks or extra objectives. Freeze the area transition, not the music.
Keep evening time separate from Canopy lighting: visiting it must not advance the
main track to night or reset accumulated scores. Pause/resume and mobile rotation
must work throughout both areas and transition.

BUILD ORDER
1. Fit the barrier opening and entrance geometry to the cul-de-sac. Check vehicle
   clearance, route width and ground support before dressing it.
2. Build and light the complete Canopy composition, with concrete, furniture,
   umbrella, CD and a comfortable skating loop. Review desktop and phone views.
3. Connect switch state, safe camera reveal, tunnel transition and return path.
4. Wire the real Secret Mixtape unlock and verify persistence, scores, audio,
   retries, all rider/board combinations and entry at varying speeds.
