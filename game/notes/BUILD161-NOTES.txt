THE TRACK 2008 — BUILD 161
Carolina landscape revision

Replaced the added broad/hanging trees with 64 newly authored upright trunks,
forked branches and irregular broadleaf clusters. Oak/hickory-inspired forms
follow the North Carolina Piedmont direction; these are game art, not exact
botanical models. The existing mixed woodland backdrop remains.

Exposed earth now mixes warm red-orange clay, brown dirt and gravel instead
of a mostly dark-brown surface. Grass blends along woodland edges and over
the central hill around the walking path, with dry/thin patches and broken
boundaries. Initial hill grass center: x=2,z=126, radii=43/64 metres.
The grass is a visual ground material. No path, collision or terrain heights
were changed, and existing off-road riding/crash rules remain unchanged.

Generic decoded THPS4 foliage and grass tiles retain the established material
style. The previous broad/hanging tree geometry is no longer used in the build.

Validation: matched straight, cul-de-sac and entrance views, hilltop/path and
woodland views. Zero shader/page errors and non-finite tree attributes. The new
64-tree foreground uses 8,960 triangles in three batches. Tree clearance is at
least 12m beyond its declared crown radius. Both terrain shaders include grass.
Gameplay source remains identical to 160; its tests are retained. Physical-phone
performance was not measured in this material/vegetation revision.
