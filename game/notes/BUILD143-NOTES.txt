BUILD143 - PINTAIL POWERSLIDE SCORING
Sally, Black and Dog on HANDMEDOWN (Pintail) can score existing hard-carve powerslides in both rope states. This is an explicit Pintail scoring exception; flips, ollies, manuals and grinds retain the previous rider/board matrix. Boss cannot score powerslides. Dog retains failed ollies and cannot flip, manual or grind; his existing speed wobble remains.
A scoring slide requires actual lateral movement >=.40m/s, existing slide engagement >=.20, >=.25 seconds of qualifying movement, and >=1m traveled. Recovery requires .25 seconds with low sideways movement, eased steering and released slide engagement. Score: 100 base + floor(20 * qualifying distance in meters), bonus capped at 150. One combo entry per recovered slide; existing .5s bank delay and bail loss apply. Invalid ground/air state, stalled motion, direction reversal and failed recovery cancel the attempt. Physics/traction, controls and animations are unchanged.
Tests: 32 rider/board/rope-state combinations, short skid/no-slip/air/dirt/crash/stationary/reversal cases, score cap and loss before banking, and six full left/right powerslides using actual traction and yaw. Full regression: 16 files, 25 runner entries pass. Browser evidence is in validation143/. No new THPS4 reverse-engineering claims: this is Track-specific scoring atop the inherited Pintail handling.
