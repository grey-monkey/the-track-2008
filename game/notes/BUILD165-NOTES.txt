Boss: Second-Hand Smoke

Boss can collect three distinct puffs from Sprad's existing periodic exhale.
Follow the smoke behind the car. The upper-left HUD shows 1 / 3, 2 / 3,
then Second-Hand Smoke with a countdown. Other riders cannot collect them.
Collected puffs disappear; expired puffs cannot count. Progress resets on a
crash or new run. Three puffs need not come from the same exhale.

Boss lets go of the rope through the usual release transition when it triggers.
The reward reuses Secret Spliff's backflip, forward ascent, and zero-to-normal
gravity over 30 seconds, on every board. It does not grant normal trick skills.
The sky receives slowly radiating rainbow bands and a gentle pulse for
30 seconds, followed by a smooth six-second fade. The painted cloud texture,
cloud shapes and sky geometry remain intact. Normal sky color returns exactly.
This reward uses rainbow sky instead of the Secret Spliff golden world tint.
New puffs cannot extend an active reward. Pause also pauses its timer.

Smoke now spawns in the fixed-step simulation, with the same world positions
used by both rendering and swept pickup detection. Six puffs per exhale,
every 26 seconds; lifetime 4.5 seconds. No render-frame-dependent collection.

rope and hazard geometry: default Boss/longboard collects puffs at 8.56,
8.74 and 8.94 seconds with seed 52. Backflip/ascent checked in live physics.
