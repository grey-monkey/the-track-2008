THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 142 - PLYWOOD CORNER

The new milestone develops the existing subdivision ramp/curb corner into a repeatable skating line, retaining the Build133 world, crew, car, palette and route. This is a local development build, not a claim of finished THPS4 production quality.

1. World and character art
Six original THPS4 diffuse surfaces decoded from the supplied disc now drive pavement, aged concrete, plywood, timber and soil. Texture identity, archive offsets and hashes are retained in authoring/THPS4-surfaces142-provenance.json. The original 64-256px texture scale, mip chains and vertex-colored scene evidence guided this treatment. New low foundation forms, timber wall frames, stacked panels and concrete pieces establish a construction corner beside the plywood; solid collision uses the same dimensions. Contact shading, sealed joints and narrow wheel marks tie the spot to the ground. New spot geometry costs five draw calls / 1,394 triangles, plus one curb-wear strip draw call. Existing neighborhood artwork and personal landmarks remain.

2. Connected movement
35 additional original THPS4 animation clips have been decoded and merged into the 55-clip bank. Turn entry/hold, crouch, push, short/deep landing and grind sequences now dispatch from the original QB evidence. Rigid joint and board-rotation blending replaces per-vertex crossfades. Source landing airtime thresholds guide absorption. Stance mirroring and foot contact remain on Track character proportions.
Sally's original extreme boardslide balance poses exceeded the garment stretch limit. The visible boardslide pose now uses the middle 60% of the source range while physics retains the full balance range. Tilted source-board foot support preserves sideways stance. Audited residual sole-plane gap is at most .0064 character-local units. This adaptation is explicitly different from the source.

3. Complete line
The existing 16.45m curb crown supports intentional 50-50s and front/back boardslides. Approach airborne and approximately along the curb; capture requires a held input, descending flight, <=30cm proximity and <=35-degree alignment. Balance, friction, graded height, distance scoring, rail-end exits, release and pop-off are integrated. Those world-scale dynamics are Track adaptations, not recovered native equations. Landing banks the combo; falling loses it.
Keyboard: G held for 50-50, B held for boardslide; A/D balance, release G/B to drop back toward the road, Space release to pop out. On touch, ollie first, then add a second finger to catch; drag sideways for a boardslide, countersteer to balance, release a finger to exit. A second finger after the ollie flick now unlocks balance steering without producing another ollie. Grounded two-finger manual is preserved.
Only Sally/Black on Element/Dogtown can grind. Rope must still be ridden and released first. Boss's obstacle/curb/ramp bails, Dog's speed wobble, novice failed ollies and the two crash choices remain.

4. Sound and camera
Existing recorded metal/wood contacts and scrape audio now distinguish entry, sustained grind/slide and exit. Wheels quiet while on the rail; scrape stops on exit and pause. Landing contact strength follows impact speed. Car and neighborhood spatial sound remain. Landscape framing uses the vertical equivalent of THPS4's scripted 72-degree horizontal field of view at 4:3; portrait opens to 65 degrees for nearby obstacles. Small damped landing compression and grind framing are Track adaptations. The camera follows travel, never a boardslide's sideways deck rotation.

Evidence and checks

Native balance-parameter dispatch was traced at 0x4d61a0. Detailed native evidence is exported in authoring/REA142-native-evidence.json. Source executable was not run in this milestone; exact native balance equations and runtime camera behavior are not claimed.
15 Node test files pass (24 runner test entries). Coverage includes all rider/board permissions, 36 driving circuits, crashes, retargeting, garment limits, input, grind capture/momentum/balance/exits and animation dispatch. Browser checks: 16 actual ramp combinations, both path mouths in both directions, desktop/portrait/landscape UI, scoring, pause and crash choices. Actual curb integration covers 16 rider/board/direction/grind cases. Continuous normal-input routes succeed for both grind types on all six driving profiles: tow, release, ramp, land, ollie, grind, exit, score. Those route tests vary the ollie timing; unsuccessful timing attempts are retained in the report rather than concealed. Controlled pose tests place the rider near the rail and are labeled separately from the continuous routes. Added turn/landing garment audit covers 221 poses; adapted grind garment audit covers 104 poses. Browser sound check confirms all recordings loaded, scrape/roll switching, pause muting and score after exit. Final screenshots were visually reviewed.

Limits / next work
One finished experimental corner does not complete the full neighborhood's art. Character silhouettes/clothing still use the supplied handoff meshes; further authored modeling and environmental depth would be needed for closer console-era production quality. Only this marked curb is grindable. Grabs, spine/quarter-pipe transitions and a larger trick vocabulary remain future work. Physical phone frame pacing and touch comfort have not been measured; browser viewport checks are not a real-device playtest. The preview and supplied original-game assets remain local and have not been published.
