THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 171
Gradual, uncapped Pintail slide scoring

Powerslides earn 20 base points per second of qualifying sideways sliding.
There is no upfront 100-point award, distance bonus, or 250-point cap.
Examples: 1 second = 20, 3 seconds = 60, 10 seconds = 200, 20 seconds = 400.
Only actual slide time accumulates points. Recovery or straight-line time
does not count. A brief skid still must meet the existing minimum duration
and distance before it qualifies. Pending HUD points use exactly the same
calculation as the final award, and bank only after a clean recovery.
Countersteering carries the same attempt, without extra multiplier awards.
A wipeout still loses the pending points. Existing combo rules remain.

Build170 speed thresholds, slide angles, grip, steering and recovery remain
unchanged. Sally, Black and Dog can score Pintail slides; Boss cannot.
There are no new controls, menus or tricks.

Validation: 47 passing gameplay tests, including rate consistency across
30/60/120Hz, long slides beyond 250, countersteering, recovery freeze,
permissions, combo preservation and bail loss. 32 before/after physics
traces match exactly. Browser checks cover gradual HUD accumulation, clean
banking and an uncapped 400-point HUD layout in portrait. The portrait
400-point sample is a UI-only fixture, not a recorded 20-second ride.
