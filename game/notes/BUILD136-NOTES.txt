THE TRACK 2008 / BUILD 136 / October 15, 2026

Corrections requested after Build135
- Removed Free skate from selection. Normal play always starts on the rope. Release is the only transition to independent riding. Flip/shove-it no longer silently releases the rope.
- Every crash ends the run, with Another run and Back to the car as the two choices. Another run returns to towing. Crashes never drop the player into free skating.
- Hard falls (speed >= 6.5 m/s, collision impulse >= 4.5 m/s, or a significant downward drop) do not play a stand-up recovery. Softer hip/back falls recover; soft rolls/trips use their existing built-in get-up. The car continues moving.
- Lower ollie impulse: 2.15/2.25/2.55/2.75 m/s for longboard/hand-me-down/cruiser/street, giving about 0.23-0.38m airborne rise.
- Recovered Sally's original MakeHuman garment weights from the earlier clothing authoring pipeline. Verified all 9,343 exported clothing vertices against original vertex/UV identity. Smoothed the connected hoodie weights across welded topology to remove abrupt shoulder/sleeve transitions. Pants and collar retain authored weights. Covered arm triangles are omitted only from Sally's articulated skin surface so elbows cannot poke through the long sleeves; hands and wrists remain visible. Replaces the erroneous short-sleeved DOG-shirt nearest-neighbour transfer.
- Removed the on-screen action pads and Sound on toggle. Sound settings remain in Help. Drag/flick/tap gestures handle steering, release, pushing, ollie, tricks, braking and manual. The score itself opens pause.
- Persistent seven-digit banked run score with a separate current combo, multiplier, trick names and bail feedback. Score remains visible through the crash-choice screen.

Validation
All seven test files pass. Includes hard/soft fall completion, no skating after crashes, rope-only start and release flow, lower jump height, trick launches and scoring, gesture cancellation, and multi-touch without unintended jumps.
