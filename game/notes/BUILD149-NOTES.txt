THE TRACK 2008 — BUILD149

Sidewalk geometry
- Straight chamfered flares narrow from4.3m at the curb to the2.1m walk, with a straight initial centerline and no hooked return.
- The first4m of each entrance are level along travel. A smooth height curve joins the existing path by18m.
- Local terrain and shoulder triangles are cut down beneath the path. Surrounding earth is retained, with a narrow blend at the pavement edge. Local subdivision follows the corridor footprint instead of adding dense triangles across a whole rectangular area.
- The regular terrain mesh omits the replaced cells; actual cut terrain triangles supply physics heights and conservative support bounds. Curb, ground, board and rider contact share the final geometry.
- Concrete joints and dark path edging retained, along with148 lighting and approved147 textures.

Secret Spliff
- Original low-poly paper-cone collectible with a dark ember end and small orbiting glints, hovering/spinning at neck height at the path midpoint.
- Once per run; a new Ride resets collection. Living rider proximity and vertical overlap are required. Crashed riders cannot collect it.
- Collecting immediately launches every rider/board with3.4m/s upward velocity. At4.905m/s2 gravity, a level-ground launch reaches about1.16m and lasts about1.38s.
- Half gravity lasts15 simulation seconds, respects pause, and returns to normal at expiry. Airborne motion, buffered landing timing and slope gravity use the active gravity value.
- Pickup launch bypasses ordinary ollie permissions and does not force a novice failed-trick landing. Existing character-specific surface hazards and ordinary trick rules remain.
- Score panel expands for Secret Spliff and a remaining-seconds readout, without covering the score or rider label. No unsolicited score bonus added.
