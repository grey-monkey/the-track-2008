BUILD 140 / CHARACTER HANDLING AND CONNECTED SKATING LINE

Boss is the least capable skater. On every board, the first grounded wheel touching a manhole, divot, patch, gray curb, or non-asphalt ground triggers a wipeout. The plywood ramp also triggers a fall at first wheel contact. Solid trick obstacles no longer inherit forgiving-board ride-over exceptions for Boss. Airborne obstacle collisions remain active. His attempted ollies still bail on landing, and he cannot perform flips or manuals.

Dog develops speed wobble on every board after riding straight above 6.3 m/s (about 14 mph) for 0.85 seconds. Alternating board yaw builds with time and speed; continued neglect causes a WOBBLE fall. Deliberate carving settles it. Slowing below the threshold or being airborne drains the build-up. Crouching alone does not cancel it. The oscillation changes actual steering and lateral forces; it cannot count as user steering and cure itself.

Free-skate idle/crouch stances now shift weight with turns, bend the knees and prepare for the plywood approach. Both feet retain their source transforms while the legs solve to the shifted pelvis, followed by the existing shoe/deck contact pass. Both stances use mirrored weight shifts. Towing, push, airborne trick and fall clips retain their existing behavior.

Connected skating-area progress: corrected support-block collisions beneath the plywood skin. A continuous input-driven test starts a tow 30 metres before the ramp, releases the rope, steers onto the plywood, lands and completes a 150-point kickflip across all six driver profiles. The test changes no rider positions or velocities after initialization. This is a scripted local simulation check, not human playtest approval. Surface checks still cover all 16 rider/board combinations: Boss bails on the ramp; the other 12 combinations launch and land.

Validation: 12 test files pass. 120 posture samples preserve exact feet/toe transforms and leg lengths. Actual rendered road fixtures checked for Boss on all four boards. Edge/WebGL browser checks cover desktop, portrait and landscape layouts, ramp flight, both path mouths in both directions, trick scoring, pause/resume and the two crash choices. Captures, reports and scripts are in validation140/. Mobile layouts were tested on desktop Edge; actual phone performance remains unverified.

The larger area/art milestone remains in progress. Next: broader line playtests, clearer environmental detail and grounded character transitions, then deeper trick interaction. This build does not claim THPS4-level visual completeness or add grinds/transition skating.
