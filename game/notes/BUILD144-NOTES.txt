THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 144 - CHARACTER AND BOARD MOTION

Based on Build143, preserving the Build133 world, controls, car routes and character rules.

VISIBLE CHANGES
- Source-driven front/back powerslide body poses, lower knees and hips, shoulders opposing the slide, bounded travel-facing head correction. The physical deck still determines heading. Boss does not get this skilled slide pose.
- Actual moving-brake animation; pushing cycles now use six stable full-body source variants rather than a single repeated cycle.
- Animated source palms and finger joints, with a corrected right-hand bind fitted to the existing Track character mesh. Existing head calibration retained after verification.
- Both feet support the actual rendered board plane after animation blending, including board pitch, stance mirroring, grinds and manuals. The pushing/braking support leg stays on the board while the other foot clears the deck before reaching terrain.
- Air turning, takeoff-to-air posture and preparation for landing, without changing jump height. Active flips retain their own board and foot tracks. Landing absorption and manuals take priority over residual slide motion.
- Removed the generic idle arm override to retain the original motion's shoulder, elbow and palm relationship.

REFERENCE AND ADAPTATION
REA workflow inspected the supplied THPS4 executable and packaged QB/animation resources. Executable SHA256: 8661edc1a3df21d897ef9119e333d84079bbdf0ad97171eed3f4ad5bd4329e5b.
Recovered 20 additional motion records (18 new names; complete bank now 73 clips). QB dispatch and exact decoded bone transforms informed this work. Source hashes and findings are retained under authoring.
Native executable string references were located in pointer tables; no native head solver or complete animation engine was recovered. The original executable was not run. This is a browser implementation and retargeting of reference motion, not a claim of exact original engine behavior.
The source powerslide is a 180-degree switch maneuver. Track uses a bounded pre-switch portion, removes its board yaw and adds a bounded gaze adaptation for the existing sustained Pintail slide. Timing, sole contact and scoring remain Track-specific.
THPS4 skitch clips use a two-handed low car grip. They were researched but do not replace Track's one-handed rope reach. Tow motion remains the existing authored system; this pass primarily improves released skating and shared hands during articulated falls.
