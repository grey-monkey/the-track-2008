THE TRACK 2008 — BUILD 160
Coordinated world and character material pass

This is the playable continuation of Build 159, applied throughout the game.

TERRAIN
Removed an older soil replacement that flattened distant ground to pink clay
and prevented later cleanup from running. Soil now uses decoded diffuse earth
and aggregate tiles with rotated sampling, broad mineral/compaction variation,
gravel near the road, and a 512x512 world field for work areas and woodland.
Terrain, sidewalk openings, road/path collision and elevations are unchanged.

ARCHITECTURE AND CONSTRUCTION
All three houses use local-metre UVs that survive geometry batching, source
masonry and asphalt-shingle textures, and restrained siding seams. Roof and
eave shade, window recesses and garage relief retain their geometric depth.
Concrete foundations share the concrete material system. The existing timber,
plywood and concrete-block piles now show more of their source surface detail.
The source tile initially tested for masonry contained a cornice band; the
final build uses a full wall tile to avoid repeating that band over the houses.

CHARACTERS AND LIGHTING
Terrain, architecture, skin and clothing use a shared warm directional light
and cool sky fill. Removed the late character-color blend that obscured fabric
detail; added subdued cloth weave and seam/cuff shading. Hair keeps strand
detail with a quieter sun sheen. Car enamel has a slightly broader reflection.
The approved sky, evening progression, minimal white UI and gameplay remain.
Build 159's 63 dimensional foreground trees and varied backdrop are retained.

REFERENCE AND SCOPE
The source materials were decoded from the supplied THPS4 College texture
archive and inspected against its scene/material evidence. Exact archive,
record offsets, resolution and PNG hashes are in
authoring/THPS4-material160-provenance.json. The tree/scene and REA executable
evidence from 159 is retained. This pass applies those material and shading
findings; it does not claim a newly recovered native retail lighting formula.
The Track retains its own site layout, characters, clothes and game rules.

VALIDATION
39 existing gameplay test entries passed with zero failures. Desktop Edge
checks compiled the actual materials and rendered four matched riding views,
all three houses, all four riders, dusk/night, and 390x844 / 844x390 viewports.
Zero shader/page errors or non-finite house geometry. Confirmed two active
terrain shaders and no remaining legacy flat-soil replacements, six facade
shader variants, and 28 cloth variants. Houses remain 14 material batches;
the 63 foreground trees remain three batches. No new terrain or physics meshes.
Phone-size layout checks are browser checks, not physical-device benchmarks.
