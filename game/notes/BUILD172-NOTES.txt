THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 172
Car albums and continuous playback

Albums in How to ride: Lo and Behold, Dude Ranch, Nevermind, In Rainbows.
The three supplied archives add 38 tracks in their supplied numerical order.
Full audio duration is preserved, including any silence and bonus material.
Audio is streamed track-by-track, not preloaded as entire albums.

Playback is attempted when the selection screen finishes loading. Browsers
that block autoplay start it on the first interaction. Music and ambient audio
continue through pause, instructions, crashes, another run and back to car.
The same album choice does not restart a track; changing albums starts the
new album at track one. Album choice is remembered between visits.
Explicit Sound off and switching away from the browser still suspend audio.

Secret Mixtape remains hidden. Its ten tracks and persistent discovery hook
are prepared, but a physical findable tape has not yet been placed in the world.
How to ride is also available while paused. Crash screens retain only
Another run and Back to the car.

Validation: 51 automated checks pass. All 38 new encoded tracks passed full
audio decoding. Browser results are in validation172/build172-browser-check.json.
Gameplay handling, camera, world and scoring are unchanged.
