THE TRACK 2008 — BUILD 156
Lumber ledge grind usability

CHANGES
- Lumber ledge catch radius increased from 30 to 42 cm; allowed approach angle increased from 35 to 50 degrees. The rider must still be airborne, descending and deliberately holding the grind input. Curbs retain their previous capture geometry.
- Off-center lumber catches start with less balance displacement, and drift builds gently during the first 0.35 seconds. Lean correction and extreme-balance wipeouts remain active.
- Balance meter has a center target, a wider white rail and directional keyboard/touch guidance when the rider drifts. No new buttons or background panels.
- Releasing a lumber grind gives a small 0.65 m/s upward hop and 12 cm step toward the road to clear the supports. Space retains the existing stronger pop. Horizontal speed is conserved and points bank only after landing.
- Sally remains the only grinding character, using Element or Dogtown. No additional tricks.

CONTROLS
Ollie alongside the lumber edge and hold G for a 50-50 or B for a boardslide. Use A/D to keep the marker centered. Release the grind key to hop off. On touch, hold two fingers while descending onto the ledge, drag to balance and lift to exit.

VALIDATION
39 automated test entries passed, including the new ledge approach/exit cases and existing character, board, balance, scoring and collision checks.
Eight full-scene runs covered both directions, centered and 38 cm off-center approaches, and full-length and early exits. Each caught the ledge, landed safely and banked points.
The five-second demo uses the actual game simulation and normal gameplay camera with scripted positioning and inputs. It is a functional demonstration, not a recording of manual play or a before/after comparison.
Real keyboard G and B events also caught the ledge, released and banked points. Portrait and landscape browser views were checked; landscape balance guidance sits to the side of the rider. Browser viewport checks do not replace testing on a physical phone.
