THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 178
Canopy revision from the supplied cul-de-sac sketch

The secret entrance sits within a circular concrete island in the cul-de-sac.
Its beveled edge rises 9 cm (about 3.5 inches) above the asphalt. Sally, Black
and Dog can ride it; Boss trips on it unless his existing smoke immunity is
active. The opened ramp joins the asphalt directly and remains usable by Boss.
The orange barrier and dirt patch sit on the island before the reveal, then
move aside. Six smooth driving lines give the car room to pass the island.

The construction trigger is now at the clay edge of the cul-de-sac. Its plank,
paint can and support retract out of the road during towing, and extend after
release into free ride. Boss can safely roll over the activation plank. The
exception does not permit tricks or make other obstacles safe.

The Canopy now has a circular knee-high concrete wall, evenly spaced warm
wall-mounted footpath lights, and a clearly marked, usable return tunnel.
The concrete floor, moonlit bistro table, four metal chairs and umbrella remain.

Mixtape discovery now accepts a complete circuit throughout the usable room,
rather than resetting outside the old narrow ring. Pausing and small course
corrections preserve progress; back-and-forth rocking does not count as a lap.
A small white CD progress meter and a subtle ring around the table show progress.
One full circuit unlocks Secret Mixtape without replacing the current music.

Validation: 71 automated checks pass, including real tight and wide circuits
with every rider/board combination, Boss's island and trigger rules, containment
and return passage, and 36 car circuits across six lines and three pace settings.
Desktop and portrait browser checks cover stowing, activation, tunnel entry,
partial progress, discovery, exit, retry, score retention and Back to the car.
