THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 177
The Canopy: construction chain reaction and Secret Mixtape

Release the rope and roll over the loose plank beside the portable generator
on the first stretch of road. It tips a paint can along scrap lumber into the
generator starter. A short camera sequence shows the reaction and the orange
cul-de-sac barrier moving aside to reveal a descending concrete passage.

Every rider can activate this flush plank without a trick, including Boss.
This exception does not change Boss's trick restrictions or normal hazards.

Skate down the passage through darkness into The Canopy: concrete pavement,
soft moonlight, a round metal bistro table, four chairs and a patio umbrella.
Complete one continuous circle around the furniture, in either direction, to
collect the CD. The discovery message and chime unlock Secret Mixtape in
Settings without restarting or replacing the album already playing.

The passage back returns you to the cul-de-sac. Retry stays in the Canopy;
Back to the car returns to selection. Character scores remain intact. The
route stays open for this evening, including rider changes and retries; a
new page session closes it. Secret Mixtape discovery uses the existing saved
album unlock. The room's furniture remains a real collision obstacle.

Reference: supplied THPS4 Alcatraz goal/door scripts and goal camera utilities
informed the gated reveal, brief camera sequence and one-time discovery.
The construction gag, Canopy models and route implementation are original.
No claim is made that this is a copied THPS4 quest or animation.

Validation: 67 automated checks, including actual steering/pushing through

CD unlock, unchanged music stream, return, retry and Back to the car.
All six driving lines clear the opened tunnel with the full car footprint.
