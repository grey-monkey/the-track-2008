# Environment study 39 — curb and drain inspection

Firsthand browser inspection covered all nine drains, western and eastern aerial views, the cul-de-sac, and the entrance. Corrected the junction drain to follow the new rounded pavement boundary instead of the former road centerline. Drain frames follow longitudinal road grade without tipping across the rolled curb profile.

The cul-de-sac and its throat now use the continuous rounded concrete profile, contraction joints and asphalt seam, replacing the narrow legacy flat curb. Circuit curb front faces are closed. Heights at the entrance, loop and junction use the road surface at each actual vertex, removing small buried sections.

Browser fixture audit: zero curb vertices below road or rendered terrain across all four curb meshes. Minimum road clearance, including bottom of concrete toes: entrance 3.16 mm, circuit 3.89 mm, junction 1.89 mm, cul-de-sac 4 mm. These minima are the bases of vertical faces, not curb crown heights. Cabinets remain seated on pads; cover and drain lips retain minimum 18 mm and 20 mm respectively.

Validation: road contact checks cover 8,048 wheel positions, all 7,077 road triangles are finite, fixture station and gate clearance checks pass. Geometry changes do not add hazard collision behavior. Local previews only; public game not republished.
