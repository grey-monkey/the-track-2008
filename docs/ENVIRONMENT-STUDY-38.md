# Environment study 38 — entrance / loop junction

Replaced the 6.5 m circular junction patch with a union of the three road arms and 6 m rounded inside returns. Shape follows the Greenbriar Hills Plantation Road / Copperleaf Commons Court aerial reference; radii are reconstructed, not surveyed.

Generated one nonoverlapping tiled asphalt mesh. The junction curbs follow its exterior, including rolled concrete, contraction joints and a narrow asphalt separation seam. Existing curb and shoulder geometry is clipped at the replacement boundary, preventing internal curb remnants. Road contact uses the visible mesh and clipped entrance apron.
