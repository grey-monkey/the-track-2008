# Environment study 40 - residential lantern streetlights

Asset: Street Lamp 01 by Josh Dean, Poly Haven.
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/street_lamp_01
License: CC0. No purchase or account needed. Downloaded through the Poly Haven API (Powered by Poly Haven).
Original approximately 31k triangles; scene version 19,896 triangles, shared geometry/materials across 10 posts, 1K diffuse texture. Glass and exposed bulb retained. Cast metal is darkened for the evening palette and each lantern has a small warm halo. Overall height is reconstructed at 4.4m, not a surveyed dimension.

Replaced all 16 procedural posts via the environment-view lamp override. Added 2 entrance posts and 8 irregularly spaced circuit posts at frontage/verge locations. No posts remain within the cul-de-sac exclusion area. The new junction post is on the inner verge, not in the entrance roadway. All posts use actual ground sampling plus a footing beneath the cast base.

References inspected firsthand: November 2025 Street View from Strawberry Lane looking into the gated entrance, whole-neighborhood aerial, close western-frontage aerial. The lantern family is an approximation of the visible traditional black fixtures, not a verified manufacturer match. Aerial imagery does not reliably resolve every pole or establish 2008 locations; placements follow the registered roads/frontages and are reconstructed where obscured.
