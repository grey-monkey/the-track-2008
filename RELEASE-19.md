# Study 19 — Individual skater crew

All four playable riders now use the same adapted Otaku character foundation, with individual proportions, clothing, hair silhouettes, and skin palettes.

- DOG: shortest and compact, dark tousled hair, loose dark tee and shorts.
- SALLY: slightly taller, broader silhouette, cropped light hair, folded black hood and cargo shorts.
- BOSS: taller than Sally and shorter than Black, pale complexion, nearly black brown hair, white tee and slim dark jeans.
- BLACK: tallest and lankiest, light swept hair, band tee and faded jeans.
- Sprad remains the seated passenger with his purple beanie, glasses, and plaid jacket.

Head and eye proportions are reduced from the trial model. Hair caps and jeans are fitted to the animated character. New seated poses support the passenger lookbacks. Driving speed selection, rope behavior, and the illustrated environment are retained.

Adapted rider geometry: Otaku by Richie Kirui, BlendKit Royalty Free license. Sprad and canvas shoes use MakeHuman CC0 assets. The original source asset is not included in this release. Existing W124 attribution remains in How to ride.
