# Study 18 — Driving pace and a DOG character trial

The home screen now has a driving-speed slider beside rope length. Easy is 60% of the previous pace, the center is the unchanged 100% pace, and Fast is 140%. Five-percent steps. Selection persists locally and carries into retries and subsequent tows. Driver differences remain; the car still eases into acceleration and braking. This changes driving targets, not simulation time. The active play screen remains free of controls and text.

DOG alone trials a new human foundation: **Otaku by Richie Kirui**, downloaded free from BlendKit. Adapted the source rig to riding, crouching, turning and release poses; added garment volume, shortened the hair and filled the uncovered crown, fitted the existing canvas shoes, and integrated matte pigment shading. Other riders and all seated passengers retain their previous bodies for comparison. This is a playable model trial, not finished crew likeness or final illustration quality.
