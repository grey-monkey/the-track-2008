THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 180
The ride, rebuilt

Riding
Steering now asks for a turn rather than a skid angle, so a held carve is an arc
across the road and keeps almost all of its speed. Holding a hard carve no
longer ends the run. The rope is a softer one-way spring with the give of an
arm; a sideways haul scrubs some speed, and slack comes up as a tug. Left alone,
the rider holds the line the Mercedes just drove; carving overrides that. A
rider overrunning a slack rope checks their speed instead of passing the bumper.

Driving
The driver accelerates harder (6 m/s in 3.9 s, was 8.5 s) and holds a bend's
speed until the skater on the rope has cleared it. Cul-de-sac speed is about
5 m/s. Wheel grip is 30 percent higher on every board.

Characters
Dog and Boss keep their speed wobble, but it now begins above normal tow pace,
so it belongs to the Fast setting. Dog's high-speed grip fade is gentler.

Controls and camera
Flick up to let go works at the end of a held carve; the same thumb steers again
without lifting; the stick floats; a held carve is never a crouch; dragging down
brakes only when free riding. The camera drops and widens with speed, leans into
carves and lurches when the rope snatches. Rope snatch and creak are audible.

Look
The final pass is multisampled (edges were not anti-aliased before) and carries
one dusk grade: bloom, indigo shadows with peach lights, contrast and vignette.
The ten-minute evening is scheduled as sunset at three minutes, afterglow, a
blue hour of over three minutes, then night. Lamp glows are larger.

Smaller changes
Score card hidden until there is a score. Garbled apostrophes repaired. A
one-line control hint on the first three rides. Only the chosen rider's motion
loads before the menu (33.6 MB before first frame, was 57.6 MB). Clay only
throws a rider above a walking roll.

Validation: 81 automated checks. Hands-off laps completed by all 16 rider/board
pairs at easy and normal pace on short, medium and long rope (Boss on the
longboard is thrown by a road patch on lap six, by his own rule). Desktop
browser play-through of tow, slalom, cul-de-sac whip, release and free ride.
Not yet checked on a physical phone.
