# Study 43 — vintage cruiser and surf pintail

The nice longboard and Element geometry are unchanged. This pass adapts the two existing CC0 elmaterdor assemblies rather than downloading another mismatched model.

- Bowl cruiser: 8.5 mm laminated deck instead of approximately 25 mm through the original middle; broad rounded nose, squarer single kicktail with a gradual rise, and shorter truck spacing.
- Hand-me-down: 98 cm surf-style pintail with rounded nose, tapered tail, 9 mm deck and detailed original hardware.
- Rider standing heights follow the thinner decks. All four board tumble contact checks pass with at least 2 mm surface clearance on the test slope. Browser close-up checks passed without errors. Actual phone review remains the user's next check.

## Sources and research

Existing downloaded models by elmaterdor, CC0: [Cruiser Board](https://blendswap.com/blend/17400), [Longboard](https://blendswap.com/blend/11386). Approved boards retain their Study 42 attribution.

The adapted cruiser is not an exact replica. No manufacturer artwork was copied.

Rejected: the Nikita Kravchenko and Kaden Cooley downloadable longboards use drop-through outlines; they do not solve the requested pintail shape. The TurboSquid pintail was $59, so it was not purchased. Cost of this pass: $0.
