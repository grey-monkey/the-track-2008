THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 174
Per-rider evening scores

Sally, Dog, Black and Boss keep independent accumulated scores for the current
open game. Returning to the car, switching riders or boards, changing stance,
and choosing Another run keep each rider's earned points.
The HUD shows the selected rider's running evening total.

Existing scoring is unchanged: only banked points count. A wipeout loses the
unfinished combo, not points earned earlier. Pending combos do not transfer
between attempts or riders.

Scores live only in this page's memory. Reloading or closing and reopening the
game starts every rider at zero. Returning to a suspended page through browser
history starts a fresh evening. Pausing, opening Settings, or temporarily
switching tabs does not erase points. Saved album/menu preferences are separate.
No new evening duration or end-of-game behavior is introduced.

Validation: 54 automated checks pass, including score isolation, retry, bank/bail
and fresh-evening tests.
Browser checks use the requested Sally 250 / Dog 500 example as seeded banked
points and exercise actual rider, board, stance, retry and reload handlers.

Play: https://grey-monkey.github.io/the-track-2008/build174/playtest174.html?v=174
Local: node serve.cjs

Plywood ramp: riding face maps one continuous veneer panel from the existing
texture atlas. No repeated sheet seams. Ramp dimensions, edge plies, support
blocks, collision and takeoff behavior are unchanged.
