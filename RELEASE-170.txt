THE TRACK 2008 — BUILD 170
Speed-sensitive Pintail drift refinement

Pintail slides for Sally, Black and Dog now require >=6.2m/s (about 14mph),
firm steering (.64), and a loaded turn sustained for .18 seconds. Gentle
carves and quick steering taps remain normal riding. The board must be
moving forward with the turn loaded in the requested direction.

Available slide angle and grip loss scale with current speed, reaching an
86.4-degree target only at high speed. As momentum fades the angle narrows,
grip returns, and the active slide ends below 4.6m/s. Steering reversal first
unwinds the previous slide instead of snapping to the opposite direction.
Normal carving has a speed-sensitive steering limit. Recovery aligns the
board with actual travel before restoring full grip. There is no road magnet
or velocity reset: entry line, curbs, obstacles and dirt still matter.

Use existing A/D or arrows, or mobile drag. Build speed, commit to a turn,
then ease off or countersteer to recover. Powerslide points bank after a
clean recovery, with the existing 100–250 point cap. No new tricks or controls.
Boss and other boards retain their previous handling exactly.

The recovered THPS4 slide poses from Build169 are retained. These sustained
drift mechanics are Track-specific adaptations, not claimed native THPS4
physics. See authoring/THPS4-pintail169-evidence.txt for source provenance.

Validation: 47 passing gameplay tests; 26 exact before/after traces for
other boards and Boss; speed/angle, deceleration, countersteering and impact
checks; real-road browser recovery and towing; desktop and portrait renders.
Physical phone performance was not measured. See validation170 for evidence.
