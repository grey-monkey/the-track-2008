THE TRACK 2008 - BUILD 176
Boss cosmetic low-speed wobble

Boss has a faint wheel wobble at low rolling speeds: one eighth of Dog's full
wobble amplitude (about 1.07 degrees either side). It fades in from slow rolling
and fades out before high-speed instability. It is absent at rest, in menus,
in flight, during crashes and while pushing or braking.

This motion affects only rendered rider/deck transforms. It cannot change
steering, traction, velocity, points or crash buildup. Rider and board rotate
together to keep the feet planted. Existing high-speed Boss wobble and smoke
immunity remain as in Build175. Existing real obstacles can still cause a fall.

Validation: 60 automated checks pass. Browser rendering confirms 0.01875-radian
visual yaw with unchanged physical rider state and no runtime errors.

The Canopy secret tunnel is a design proposal, not included in this playable
build. See Canopy-Secret-Route-Design176.txt for the agreed setting and proposed
switch, barrier reveal, dark transition and Secret Mixtape discovery sequence.
