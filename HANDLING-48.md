# Study 48: a restrained refinement of Study 47

Study 47 remains available unchanged at handling-retry.html. This preview is handling-refinement.html.

Small per-board turning adjustments: Luxury response 0.98 and turn 0.99; Pintail 1.02 / 1.015; Cruiser 1.07 / 1.025; Element 1.12 / 1.01. Multipliers apply to the existing steering, not a replacement solver. The 1.3 rad/s heading ceiling remains.

Carves retain 8% of the speed removed by lateral alignment (rejected Study 45 retained 90%). This cannot raise speed above its pre-contact value, is disabled in the air and dirt, and fades out during sliding. Rope damping changes from 0.70 to 0.68; spring frequency remains 32. Existing brakes, release behavior, hazards, ollies, and performance optimizations remain.

Validation: 15 board/hazard personality checks, 14 detailed hazard checks, bounded-energy checks and eight paired Study 47/48 rope rides. Ordinary test carving produces no slide assist. The paired open-loop rides retain the same riding/crashed outcomes; peak speeds differ by less than 1.1%, rope extension remains below 2.3 cm. This does not replace phone playtesting.
